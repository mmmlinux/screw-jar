Screw Jar

Pours rigid-body parts into an open box and counts how many end up entirely inside it. Pour one kind of part or a mix: build the list under Parts to pour, or fill it with random sizes from the screw jar. Choose the bin under Add parts and settings. Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Pours until heaped, shakes to settle, scrapes off anything above the rim, then tops up and repeats.

Strike off removes every part that pokes above the rim, like levelling with a straight edge.

These take effect straight away, even mid-pour. Pour rate is for the Pour button. Shaking settles the pile so more fits; half a second to a second gets most of the benefit, and 0 turns it off. Freezing settled parts speeds up bins too big to fill by locking parts buried under the surface once they stop moving. It isn't used when filling to the rim, because it makes those counts a few percent low.

      Sizes in

      Use the bin's real shape, walls and all. Parts collide with the mesh itself, and count as inside when they're in its hollow and below the level it would fill to without spilling, so dividers, compartments and a low front all work. Press Apply and empty to use it.

      Pouring

      Pouring only the max shows the real bin part-filled and how high that many parts reach. Scaling up gives an estimate for a full bin. A higher max is more accurate but slower. Zinc-plated steel on steel is roughly 0.3 to 0.4 friction.
